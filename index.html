Where Technology, Science and Purpose Meet

Lomvi Robotics transforms bio-inspired engineering into non-invasive marine discovery. By replacing intrusive propellers with bird-like flapping wings, we empower researchers to observe aquatic life sustainably, silently, and without disturbing marine ecosystems.

Our Vision

From prototype to mission-ready system, Lomvi is redefining how we explore underwater environments, push the boundaries of soft robotics, and protect ocean life.

Our Technology

Guillemot-inspired flapping wings, compliant soft-body structures, and onboard autonomous navigation-seamlessly integrated for agile, silent motion underwater.

[Innovation Beneath the Surface]

[In the depths of our oceans, exploration comes with unique challenges. Pressure, fragile ecosystems, limited access, and unpredictable underwater conditions demand a new approach to robotics. We develop sustainable soft-robotic technologies designed to move, explore, and interact with underwater environments with minimal impact. Inspired by the flexibility and adaptability of nature, our systems are built to navigate delicate ecosystems, operate efficiently, and perform tasks where traditional rigid machines fall short. From deep-sea exploration to environmental monitoring and conservation, we are rethinking how technology can work with the ocean-not against it.]
